# Card Scheme Partner Meeting

## Attendees

The meeting was attended by the department head, the service owners, a risk representative and the note taker.

## Discussion

The group reviewed the current position on the card scheme partnership. The card scheme agreed to send gateway change notices five days ahead. Attendees discussed the impact on customers and on the teams that support them.

## Decisions

Both sides will test the secondary route together every month. Escalation contacts were updated for out-of-hours incidents. The chair confirmed that these decisions would be shared with the wider team.

## Actions and next meeting

Each action was given a single owner and a due date. Owners will report progress at the next meeting. The next meeting will review the card scheme partnership again, using updated figures.

## Background

The chair opened the meeting by reminding everyone of the goal: to make a clear decision and to leave with owned actions. Papers were shared before the meeting, so the time was spent on discussion rather than on presenting material. The group first checked the actions from the previous meeting, and any that were overdue were given a new date and a reason. The current position on the card scheme partnership was summarised by the service owner.

## Risks raised

The risk representative asked how the proposal would affect customers if it did not go to plan, and what the fallback would be. Attendees agreed that the largest risks should be added to the department risk register, each with an owner. Some questions could not be answered in the meeting, so the note taker recorded them for follow-up. The group agreed that any new risk to customers would be escalated rather than held until the next meeting.

## Communication

The chair asked each service owner to brief their team on the outcome of this meeting within the week. A short summary about the card scheme partnership will be posted on the department's channel, so that people who did not attend can see what was decided and why. These notes are stored in the department's document library.
