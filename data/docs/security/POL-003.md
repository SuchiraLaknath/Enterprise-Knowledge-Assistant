# Cryptographic Key Management Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out how Commercial Bank manages cryptographic key management. It gives staff a clear and consistent standard, so that decisions do not depend on individual interpretation. It supports the bank's obligations to customers, regulators and employees.

## Scope

The policy applies to all permanent staff, contractors and third parties who work with the bank's systems or information. Where a local procedure conflicts with this policy, this policy takes priority until the conflict is resolved by the policy owner.

## Requirements

TLS certificates must be listed in the central certificate inventory. Certificates for payment links are renewed at least 30 days before expiry. Private keys for payment systems are held in hardware security modules. Managers are expected to make sure that their teams understand these requirements and can show that they follow them.

## Responsibilities

The policy owner keeps this document current and answers questions about it. Line managers apply the policy in their teams and report any breaches. Every member of staff must follow the policy in their daily work.

## Exceptions and review

Any exception to this policy needs written approval from the policy owner. Approved exceptions are recorded and reviewed at the agreed date. This policy is reviewed at least once a year, and after any significant incident that involves cryptographic key management.

## Definitions and principles

In this policy, staff means anyone who works for the bank, whether permanent, temporary or contracted. The policy owner is the senior manager named on the policy register. Three principles guide every decision about cryptographic key management: protect customers first, keep a clear record of what was decided, and make the right action the easy one. When the policy does not cover a situation, staff should apply these principles and ask the policy owner for guidance before acting. Guidance given in this way is recorded, so that repeated questions can lead to an improvement in the policy itself.

## Compliance and consequences

Compliance with this policy is checked through management reviews and periodic internal audit. Audit findings about cryptographic key management are reported to the relevant risk committee, with an owner and a date for each corrective action. A breach may lead to retraining, a formal warning or, in serious cases, disciplinary action under the bank's employment procedures. Staff who raise a genuine concern in good faith are protected from retaliation, even if the concern turns out to be unfounded.

## Training and communication

New staff are introduced to this policy during induction. All staff confirm each year that they have read the current version. When the policy changes, the policy owner publishes a short summary of what has changed and why, so that teams can update their local procedures. Questions about cryptographic key management can be raised with a line manager or the policy owner at any time.
