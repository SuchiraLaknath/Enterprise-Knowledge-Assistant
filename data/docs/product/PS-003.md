# Virtual Debit Card Specification

## Overview

This specification describes virtual debit cards for Commercial Bank customers. It defines the behaviour that product, design and engineering teams have agreed.

## Customer problem

Customers want to complete everyday banking tasks without visiting a branch or calling the contact centre. Research showed that unclear steps and missing feedback are the main reasons customers give up.

## Functional requirements

Customers can create a virtual card instantly in the app. Each virtual card has its own spending limit. The card can be frozen or cancelled without calling the contact centre. All customer-facing text must be available in the bank's supported languages.

## Non-functional requirements

The feature must meet the mobile app's availability and response time objectives. Every customer action must be recorded for audit and support. The feature must meet the accessibility standard for all digital channels.

## Rollout and open questions

The rollout starts with staff, then a small customer pilot, then all customers. Success is measured by completion rate and contact centre calls about virtual debit cards. Open questions are tracked by the product owner and reviewed each fortnight.

## User journey

The customer opens the mobile app, signs in with their usual credentials and chooses the feature from the home screen. Each screen asks for one decision, explains why any information is needed, and shows clear progress towards completing the task. Before anything is confirmed, the customer sees a summary and can go back to change it. After completion, the customer receives a confirmation that stays in their activity history, which reduces calls to the contact centre about virtual debit cards.

## Error handling

If a request fails, the app explains what happened in plain language and tells the customer what they can do next. A failed action must never leave the customer unsure whether money has moved, so the app always shows the real status of the transaction. If a dependent service is unavailable, the feature is disabled with a short message rather than failing halfway through. Retrying an action must be safe, so a repeated request never creates a duplicate payment.

## Dependencies and risks

The feature depends on the mobile app platform, the customer profile service and the notification service. The fraud risk team reviews virtual debit cards before the pilot, and the compliance team confirms that it meets current regulation. The main delivery risk is integration testing with other teams, so the plan includes a shared test window. The main customer risk is confusion during the first use, which the pilot is designed to measure.
